# 8ft Maker Station: Multi-Tier 3D Printer and Soldering Workstation

A heavy-duty, vibration-damped 8-foot wide multi-tier workstation engineered specifically for high-inertia bed-slinging 3D printers (Elegoo Neptune 3 Max) and precision electronics soldering.

## Design Highlights

* **Primary Purpose**: Combined high-speed 3D printing bay and precision electronics soldering laboratory.
* **Footprint**: 96.0 inches wide by 30.0 inches deep by 78.75 inches total height.
* **Primary Work Surface**: Finished height 30.0 inches from floor (standard ergonomic bench height).
* **Short-Ways Post Orientation (3.5 in X by 1.5 in Y)**:
  * Both rear upright posts and front legs are oriented short-ways from front to back.
  * Their broad 3.5-inch faces oppose left-to-right bed-slinging inertial forces, providing 5.4 times higher moment of inertia against sideways racking.
  * Reclaims 2.0 inches of usable main benchtop depth compared to edge-on posts.
  * Provides a broad 3.5-inch fastening face for all 96-inch longitudinal rails, eliminating narrow-edge screw splitting.
* **Flush-Capped Upper Tier**: 18.0 inches deep by 96.0 inches wide at 78.75 inches finished height, providing 48.0 inches of open vertical clearance above the main deck. The rear 2x4 upright posts terminate flush at 78.0 inches directly underneath the 3/4-inch plywood top.
* **Zero Collision Framing**: Every single component occupies a mutually exclusive coordinate bounding box. Longitudinal rails and depth stringers butt squarely with zero wood intersection.
* **100% Overhead Filament Feed (Both Rows Strictly Above Gantry)**:
  * Neptune 3 Max Gantry Top: 59.5 inches from floor (30.0 in desk + 29.5 in printer).
  * **Row 1 (Lower Spool Rod)**: 63.5 inches elevation (4.0 inches above gantry top).
  * **Row 2 (Upper Spool Rod)**: 71.0 inches elevation (11.5 inches above gantry top).
  * Spool rods sit at Y = 21.0 inches, feeding filament directly down into the printer top runout sensor with zero sharp bend angles or mechanical friction.
* **Modular Spool Rods (Per Side)**: Symmetrical cradle brackets mounted directly to the 3.5-inch front face of the posts on Left, Center, and Right bents support 1 or 2 modular 48.0-inch 3/4-inch EMT conduit pipes per bay.
* **Bottom Slat Deck (Approach B)**: 4 uncut 96.0-inch 2x4 boards laid flat across the rear half of the bottom tier, providing ventilated storage for power supplies, UPS battery backups, and dry boxes.
* **Ergonomic Footwell & Elevated Footrest**: The front 11.5 inches of the bottom tier remains open for legroom and stool stowage, with the front 96.0-inch bottom stretcher functioning as an elevated footrest.

## Assembly Sequence

1. **Pre-Assemble the Three Bents**:
   * Build the Left, Center, and Right bents on the floor.
   * Attach the 25.75-inch front leg (3.5 in wide) to the 25.5-inch main deck stringer and 24.0-inch bottom stringer.
   * Fasten the assembly to the 78.0-inch rear upright post (3.5 in wide).
   * Attach the 13.5-inch upper cantilever stringer between the upper front rail position and rear post at 74.5 inches elevation.
   * Fasten dual-row conduit cradle brackets at 63.5 inches and 71.0 inches elevation to the 3.5-inch front face of the posts.
2. **Join Longitudinal Rails**:
   * Stand the three bents upright.
   * Fasten the 96.0-inch front rail and rear rail across the main deck frame.
   * Fasten the 96.0-inch lower front stretcher (footrest) and lower rear stretcher at 3.5 inches elevation.
   * Fasten the 96.0-inch upper shelf front and rear rails.
3. **Install Intermediate Ribs & Bottom Slats**:
   * Fasten the two 25.5-inch intermediate ribs under the main deck at X=24.0 in and X=72.0 in.
   * Lay the 4 uncut 96.0-inch 2x4 slats flat on top of the bottom stringers along the rear half with 0.5-inch spacing.
4. **Mount Plywood Decks**:
   * Fasten the 30.0 in x 96.0 in main benchtop from underneath using 1-1/4 inch screws into the frame.
   * Fasten the 18.0 in x 96.0 in upper shelf onto the upper frame, capping cleanly over the rear posts.
5. **Install Conduit Spool Rods**:
   * Drop the 48.0-inch 3/4-inch EMT steel conduit pipes into the lower (63.5 in) and/or upper (71.0 in) cradle brackets.
