# Design 2: GTA VI Freestanding Layered Desk Statue

A non-illuminated, freestanding multi-layer desktop art piece engineered for precision laser cutting on the xTool S1 20W, inspired by the Kaseya Center monument and multi-tier physical logo models.

---

## Dimensional Envelope & Sizing

* **Statue Width**: 280.0 mm (11.02 inches)
* **Statue Height**: 220.0 mm (8.66 inches)
* **Pedestal Footprint**: 180.0 mm (W) x 60.0 mm (D) x 6.0 mm (H)
* **Total Depth (Assembled)**: 60.0 mm (at base) / 18.0 mm (statue relief stack)
* **xTool S1 Compatibility**: Every component cuts effortlessly on standard 12" x 12" or 12" x 20" sheet stock.

---

## Layer Architecture & Mechanical Assembly (Relief Stack)

```
[ LAYER 1: BASE PEDESTAL ]  --> Dual-layer 6.0mm interlocking slotted base foot
      │
[ LAYER 2: SOLID BACKING ]  --> Solid "VI" backer board (Spray-painted sunset gradient)
      │
[ LAYER 3: PALM SILHOUETTE] --> Deep navy / matte black laser-cut palm tree fronds
      │
[ LAYER 4: "VI" BORDER ]    --> Crisp white / silver outer contour frame
      │
[ LAYER 5: LOGO EMBLEM ]    --> Raised 3D stacked "grand theft auto" lettering badge
```

### 1. Layer 1: Slotted Pedestal Foot
* **Material**: 6.0 mm Baltic Birch or dual-layer laminated 3.0 mm MDF.
* **Joinery**: Precision mortise slots matched to the bottom tenons of the "VI" uprights with 0.10 mm kerf compensation for a tight friction fit (zero glue required, or light PVA/wood glue).

### 2. Layer 2: Sunset Gradient Backplate
* **Material**: 3.0 mm Baltic Birch or MDF.
* **Finish**: Spray-painted or blended acrylic sunset gradient:
  * Top: Twilight purple / deep violet.
  * Middle: Hot neon magenta / pink.
  * Bottom: Warm golden amber / sunset orange.
* **Function**: Provides the solid colored canvas that shines through the silhouette cutouts.

### 3. Layer 3: Laser-Cut Palm Tree Silhouettes
* **Material**: 3.0 mm MDF or plywood.
* **Finish**: Matte Black or deep Midnight Navy.
* **Geometry**: Detailed fronds positioned across the interior facets of the "V" and "I", replicating the organic foliage shadows of the official game artwork.

### 4. Layer 4: "VI" Contour Accent Frame
* **Material**: 3.0 mm MDF, plywood, or gloss white acrylic.
* **Finish**: Crisp White, Off-White, or Brushed Silver.
* **Function**: Defines the iconic angular perimeter of the Roman numeral "VI", locking the silhouette layers into a clean architectural border.

### 5. Layer 5: Foreground "grand theft auto" 3D Badge
* **Material**:
  * Base backer: 3.0 mm Black acrylic or black-painted wood outline.
  * Top letters: 3.0 mm White acrylic or painted white wood letter faces.
* **Mounting**: Glued directly across the central waist of the "VI", elevated above the palm layers to provide tangible physical relief.
